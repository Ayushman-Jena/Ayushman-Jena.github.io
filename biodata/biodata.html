Bio

Ayushman Jena is a Junior Research Fellow in the Department of Instrumentation and Control Engineering at the National Institute of Technology Tiruchirappalli. He received his B.Tech. degree in Electrical Engineering from Veer Surendra Sai University of Technology, Burla.

His research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, control systems, machine learning, and physical artificial intelligence. His work focuses on developing intelligent systems that combine mathematical modeling, perception, learning, and feedback control to enable autonomous physical systems.

His technical background spans nonlinear and model predictive control, robotic systems, embedded systems, machine learning, signal processing, numerical simulation, and computational modeling. He has also worked on experimental robotic scanning systems and ultrasonic sensing platforms.

Selected Projects

ROBOTICS

Nonlinear Model Predictive Control of a Robotic Manipulator

Design and experimental validation of a nonlinear model predictive controller for a gantry robotic manipulator, incorporating dynamic modeling and constrained predictive control.

ROBOTIC SENSING

Automated Ultrasonic C-Scan System

Development of a three-axis robotic scanning platform using Arduino-based motion control, stepper motor drivers, hydrophone measurements and automated data acquisition.

MACHINE LEARNING

Physics-Informed Beam Reconstruction

Investigation of interpolation and neural-network-based super-resolution methods for reconstructing spatial acoustic fields from sparse measurements.

SPACE SYSTEMS
