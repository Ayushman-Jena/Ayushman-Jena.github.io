The Transcranial Focused Ultrasound Stimulation device is a noninvasive neurostimulation device similar to TDCS, TACS, TMS etc. The project was proposed for enhancement of cognitive abilities in military scenario, but using focused ultrasound to stimulate deep brain regions. The proposal recieved developmental funds from DRDO govt of India.
